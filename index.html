Can Tracker

Log every energy drink and coffee in one tap. See how much caffeine is in you right now, when you will be clear for bed, and what your habit really costs.

Coming soon to iPhone

One tap logging

One big button logs your usual can. Coffee takes a tap too. Light haptics and a short sound confirm it.

Caffeine, live

A curve shows how much caffeine is active in your body and when it drops below a sleep-friendly level.

Bedtime check

Set your bedtime and get an optional reminder before it is too late for another can.

Your habit in numbers

Sugar, calories, water and money spent, including the Dutch statiegeld deposit.

Compare

See how your intake compares with typical adults in the Netherlands or the United States, with the source shown.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your log stays on your iPhone.

Free, with one optional unlock

Logging and the caffeine curve are free. Can Tracker Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, that adds insights, medals, custom cans and extra app icons.

Can Tracker gives general information and estimates, not medical advice. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with any drink brand.
