Privacy Policy

Can Tracker · Last updated 13 November 2026

Can Tracker (“the app”) is made by Kasper Urdal, trading as Urdal Labs, Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Contact: urdallabs@gmail.com.

The short version

We do not collect your data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you log stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores, and where

Your drink log, your settings (such as your daily limit, bedtime and chosen comparison country) and your preferences are saved in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not use the internet to transmit your information.

Notifications

If you switch on the optional bedtime reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device. They are created locally and contain no personal data. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings at any time.

Purchases

Can Tracker Pro, the optional one-time unlock, is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. No third party receives data from the app.

Health information

Caffeine, sugar and calorie figures are estimates based on typical label values and a general 5-hour caffeine half-life. The app does not read from or write to Apple Health. It gives general information and is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about caffeine if you are pregnant, nursing, or have a health condition.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can remove individual entries inside the app, and you can erase everything by deleting the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover your data if you delete the app or lose your phone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase on your behalf. If you have any questions about your data or this policy, write to urdallabs@gmail.com. If you are in the European Union you may also contact your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds an optional iCloud backup, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date before the change takes effect.
