Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app, tap the Can Tracker Pro card, then choose “Restore purchase”. You need to be signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I change the app icon?

Open Settings, then App icon. The default icon is free. The alternate icons come with Can Tracker Pro.

Where is my data, and can I move it to a new phone?

Your log is stored only on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy in this version, so deleting the app or replacing the phone without a device transfer removes it. Restoring an iPhone backup or using Apple’s device transfer brings the app data with it.

How are caffeine and sugar worked out?

Caffeine uses typical label values per can and a general 5-hour half-life. Sugar and calories use typical label values per 100 ml. These are estimates, not exact measurements, and not medical advice.

What do the “Compare with” numbers mean?

They show typical adult intake in the country you choose, with the source and what it measures shown in Settings. The Dutch figure is a median from the Dutch national food survey. The US figure is a mean among people who consume caffeine, from drinks only.

The bedtime reminder does not arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed for Can Tracker in iOS Settings, and that the reminder is switched on in the app’s Settings.

Is Can Tracker affiliated with any drink brand?

No. It is an independent app. Flavor names in the app are original and made up.
